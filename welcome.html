What happens next

1

Within 1 working day — we reach out

A QuickCart specialist calls or WhatsApps you to understand how your business runs: outlets, staff, what you sell, how you pay.

2

We recommend your modules

Together we choose only the modules you need — POS, accounting, inventory, HR, hotel, hospital, lounge and more. Each is priced separately so you control the cost.

3

You receive an invoice

We send an invoice by email or WhatsApp. You pay by bank transfer, Moniepoint, OPay or any bank you prefer.

4

We activate your account

Once payment is confirmed we create your account, switch on your modules and send your login details.

5

You log in & change your password

Go to Settings → Security to set your own password and create passwords for your staff. We can help you import products and train your team.

While you wait

  • List your products with prices and opening stock (Excel is fine).
  • Decide which outlets and staff roles you need.
  • Prepare your bank details for receipts and invoices.
  • Browse the feature list and pricing to prepare your questions.
